PURPOSE

This document provides project-level guidance concerning student conduct and behaviour.

RESPECTFUL CONDUCT

Students are expected to communicate respectfully with instructors, academy staff and other students.

Students should avoid behaviour that disrupts learning activities or prevents other students from participating effectively.

LEARNING ENVIRONMENT

Students are expected to contribute to a learning environment that supports participation, cooperation and constructive communication.

Students should follow reasonable instructions provided by instructors or programme administrators.

ACADEMIC INTEGRITY

Students are expected to submit their own work and comply with applicable academic integrity requirements.

Students should not present another person's work as their own.

Students should not misrepresent information concerning their academic work or assessment submissions.

COMMUNICATION

Students should use appropriate academy communication and support channels when raising questions or concerns.

Students should provide accurate and relevant information when requesting assistance.

DISRUPTIVE BEHAVIOUR

Students should not intentionally disrupt classes, learning sessions, assessments or other programme activities.

Concerns concerning disruptive behaviour should be directed to the appropriate academy support or programme administration channel.

CONDUCT QUESTIONS

Students who have questions about conduct expectations should contact the appropriate academy support or programme administration channel.
