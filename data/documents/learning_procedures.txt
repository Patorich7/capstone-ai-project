PURPOSE

This document provides project-level guidance concerning student learning procedures.

COURSE PARTICIPATION

Students are expected to participate in the learning activities associated with their programme.

Students should review the learning materials provided for their course and follow instructions given by their instructor or programme administrator.

LEARNING ACTIVITIES

Learning activities may include:

1. Lectures
2. Practical exercises
3. Assignments
4. Tests
5. Projects
6. Discussions
7. Other programme-related activities

LEARNING MATERIALS

Students should use the learning materials provided for their programme to support their studies.

When students have difficulty understanding a learning material, they should seek clarification from their instructor or the appropriate academy support channel.

ASSIGNMENTS

Students should complete assignments according to the instructions provided by their instructor or programme administrator.

Students should submit assignments through the required submission channel.

PRACTICAL EXERCISES

Students may be required to complete practical exercises as part of their learning programme.

Students should follow the instructions provided for each practical exercise.

PROJECTS

Students may be required to complete projects related to their programme.

Project requirements and submission instructions should be obtained from the appropriate instructor or programme administrator.

INSTRUCTOR SUPPORT

Students who require clarification concerning course content or learning activities should contact their instructor or the appropriate academy support channel.

LEARNING DIFFICULTIES

Students experiencing difficulties that affect their ability to participate in learning activities should contact the appropriate academy support channel as soon as reasonably possible.
