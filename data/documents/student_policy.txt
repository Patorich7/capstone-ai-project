PURPOSE

This document provides general project-level guidance concerning student responsibilities and participation at Torilo Academy.

STUDENT RESPONSIBILITIES

Students are expected to:

1. Attend scheduled learning sessions.
2. Participate in programme activities.
3. Complete required learning activities and assessments.
4. Follow reasonable instructions provided by instructors or programme administrators.
5. Submit their work through the required submission channel.
6. Maintain appropriate academic conduct.
7. Ask for clarification when they do not understand an instruction.
8. Contact academy support when they experience problems affecting their programme participation.

ACADEMIC CONDUCT

Students are expected to conduct themselves appropriately during learning activities and interactions with instructors, staff and other students.

Students should communicate respectfully and follow applicable academy procedures.

ACADEMIC INTEGRITY

Students should submit their own work and should not present another person's work as their own.

Students are expected to comply with applicable academic integrity requirements.

USE OF LEARNING MATERIALS

Students should use programme learning materials for their intended educational purpose.

Students should follow any applicable instructions concerning the use, sharing or reproduction of learning materials.

COMMUNICATION

Students should use the designated academy communication or support channels when requesting assistance.

Students should provide sufficient information to help the academy understand their question or concern.

SUPPORT REQUESTS

Students experiencing difficulties with attendance, assessments, learning activities or programme participation should contact the appropriate academy support channel.
