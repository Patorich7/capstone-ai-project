PROGRAMME NAME

Product Management

PROGRAMME DURATION

The Product Management programme runs for 12 weeks.

PROGRAMME DESCRIPTION

Product Management focuses on planning, developing, launching and improving digital products.

Students learn how to understand customer needs, define product requirements, prioritize features, work with development and design teams, and evaluate product performance.

PROGRAMME TOPICS

The Product Management programme covers:

- Introduction to Product Management
- Product Strategy
- Customer Research
- User Personas
- Product Discovery
- Requirements Gathering
- Product Roadmapping
- Feature Prioritization
- Agile Product Development
- Working with Engineering and Design Teams
- Product Metrics and Analytics
- Product Launch and Growth

LEARNING FORMATS

The Product Management programme is available in all three academy learning formats:

- Physical classes
- Online classes
- Hybrid classes

SUITABILITY

Students who are interested in managing digital products, understanding customer needs, defining product requirements, planning product development and coordinating product teams may consider the Product Management programme.

The programme is particularly suitable for students who want to focus on managing products rather than primarily building software.
