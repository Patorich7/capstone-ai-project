ABOUT TORILO ACADEMY

Torilo Academy is a technology training institution in Nigeria that helps people build practical digital skills for careers in the technology industry.

CORE TRAINING AREAS

Torilo Academy provides technology and digital skills training in areas including:

- Software Development
- Product Design (UI/UX)
- Data Analysis
- Cloud Computing
- Digital Marketing
- Graphic Design
- Motion Graphics
- Web Design
- AI Engineering
- Product Management

LEARNING FORMATS

Torilo Academy provides learning through three formats:

- Physical classes
- Online classes

These learning formats are designed to support students, career switchers, entrepreneurs and working professionals.
