PURPOSE

This document provides project-level guidance concerning student attendance.

ATTENDANCE EXPECTATION

Students are expected to attend scheduled classes, learning sessions and other programme activities applicable to their course.

ATTENDANCE RECORDS

Attendance may be recorded by the academy or programme instructor.

Students should ensure that their attendance is properly recorded where an attendance process is provided.

ABSENCE

A student who is unable to attend a scheduled learning session should notify the appropriate academy support or programme channel as soon as reasonably possible.

Students should provide a reason for an absence when requested.

REPEATED ABSENCE

Repeated absence may affect a student's ability to follow course content and complete learning activities.

Students experiencing circumstances that may affect their continued participation should contact academy support.

ATTENDANCE QUESTIONS

For questions concerning a student's individual attendance record, the student should contact the appropriate academy support channel.
