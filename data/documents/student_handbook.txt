PURPOSE

This handbook provides general project-level guidance for students participating in Torilo Academy programmes.

STUDENT RESPONSIBILITIES

Students are expected to:

1. Attend scheduled classes and learning sessions.
2. Participate in programme activities.
3. Complete assignments, tests, practical exercises and projects.
4. Follow instructions provided by instructors or programme administrators.
5. Submit required learning activities through the designated channel.
6. Maintain appropriate academic conduct.
7. Seek clarification when they do not understand a learning or assessment instruction.
8. Contact academy support when they experience problems affecting their participation.

LEARNING PARTICIPATION

Students should actively participate in learning activities associated with their programme.

Students are encouraged to review learning materials, complete assigned activities and ask questions when clarification is required.

COMMUNICATION

Students should use the communication channels provided by the academy or programme administrator for questions concerning their programme.

Students should provide accurate information when requesting assistance.

ASSESSMENTS

Students may be required to complete assignments, tests, practical exercises, projects and other programme-related assessments.

Students should follow the submission instructions provided for their programme.

ATTENDANCE

Students are expected to attend scheduled classes, learning sessions and other programme activities applicable to their course.

A student who is unable to attend a scheduled learning session should notify the appropriate academy support or programme channel as soon as reasonably possible.

ACADEMIC INTEGRITY

Students are expected to submit their own work and comply with applicable academic integrity requirements.

Students should not misrepresent another person's work as their own.

STUDENT SUPPORT

Students who experience difficulties with their programme, learning activities, attendance or assessments should contact the appropriate academy support channel.
