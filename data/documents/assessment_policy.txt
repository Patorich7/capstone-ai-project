PURPOSE

This document provides project-level guidance concerning student assessments.

ASSESSMENT ACTIVITIES

Students may be required to complete learning activities such as:

1. Assignments
2. Tests
3. Practical exercises
4. Projects
5. Other programme-related assessments

ASSESSMENT PARTICIPATION

Students are expected to participate in assessments associated with their programme and follow the instructions provided by their instructor or programme administrator.

SUBMISSION

Students should submit assessments according to the instructions and submission procedures provided for their programme.

Students should ensure that their submitted work is complete and submitted through the required channel.

LATE SUBMISSIONS

A student who expects difficulty meeting an assessment deadline should contact the appropriate instructor or academy support channel as soon as possible.

The applicable instructor or programme administrator may provide further guidance concerning the situation.

ASSESSMENT QUESTIONS

Students who do not understand an assessment instruction should seek clarification from their instructor or the appropriate academy support channel.

ACADEMIC INTEGRITY

Students are expected to submit their own work and comply with applicable academic integrity requirements.

Students should not misrepresent another person's work as their own.

ASSESSMENT RESULTS

Questions concerning individual assessment results should be directed to the appropriate instructor or academy support channel.
