TORILO ACADEMY SUPPORT PROCEDURES

DOCUMENT STATUS
Authority: Synthetic project document
Status: Approved for capstone demonstration
Document Type: Support Procedures

PURPOSE

This document provides project-level guidance concerning student support procedures.

WHEN TO CONTACT SUPPORT

Students should contact the appropriate academy support channel when they need assistance with matters such as:

1. Course information
2. Learning activities
3. Assessments
4. Attendance
5. Payments
6. Refund questions
7. Programme participation
8. Other academy-related concerns

COURSE QUESTIONS

Students who have questions about available courses, course duration or course fees should consult the approved course catalog or contact the appropriate academy support channel.

The chatbot should use the approved course catalog when answering questions about course information.

LEARNING SUPPORT

Students who have difficulty understanding learning materials or learning activities should contact their instructor or the appropriate academy support channel.

ASSESSMENT SUPPORT

Students who do not understand an assessment instruction should seek clarification from their instructor or the appropriate academy support channel.

Students who experience difficulty meeting an assessment deadline should contact the appropriate instructor or academy support channel as soon as reasonably possible.

ATTENDANCE SUPPORT

Students who are unable to attend a scheduled learning session should notify the appropriate academy support or programme channel as soon as reasonably possible.

Questions concerning an individual attendance record should be directed to the appropriate academy support channel.

PAYMENT SUPPORT

Students who have questions about course fees, payment procedures or payment status should contact the appropriate academy support or programme administration channel.

REFUND SUPPORT

Students who have questions about refund eligibility, refund processing or refund status should contact the appropriate academy support or programme administration channel.

ESCALATION

If a student's question cannot be answered using the approved chatbot knowledge base, the student should be directed to the appropriate academy support channel.

The chatbot should not invent information that is not contained in the approved knowledge base.

SUPPORT REQUEST INFORMATION

When contacting support, students should provide sufficient information about their question or concern to help the academy understand the issue.

PROCEDURE LIMITATION

This is a synthetic project document created for the Torilo Academy AI Engineering Student Assistant Chatbot capstone demonstration.

It should not be presented as an official published Torilo Academy support procedure unless official documentation is subsequently provided.
