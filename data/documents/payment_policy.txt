DOCUMENT STATUS
Authority: Synthetic project document
Status: Approved for capstone demonstration
Document Type: Student Policy

PURPOSE

This document provides project-level guidance concerning course fees and student payments.

COURSE FEES

Course fees should be determined from the approved Torilo Academy course catalog contained in the chatbot knowledge base.

The chatbot should use the approved course catalog when answering questions about course fees.

The chatbot must not invent or modify course fees that are not contained in the approved course catalog.

PAYMENT INFORMATION

Students who have questions about payment procedures should contact the appropriate academy support or programme administration channel.

Students should confirm payment instructions before making a payment.

PAYMENT CONFIRMATION

Students should retain evidence of payment where applicable.

Questions concerning whether a payment has been received or recorded should be directed to the appropriate academy support or programme administration channel.

PAYMENT QUESTIONS

Students who are uncertain about a course fee, payment instruction or payment status should contact the appropriate academy support channel.

The chatbot should provide course fee information from the approved course catalog and should not invent payment instructions.

REFUND-RELATED QUESTIONS

Questions concerning refunds should be handled using the approved refund policy contained in the chatbot knowledge base.

POLICY LIMITATION

This is a synthetic project document created for the Torilo Academy AI Engineering Student Assistant Chatbot capstone demonstration.

It should not be presented as an official published Torilo Academy payment policy unless official documentation is subsequently provided.
