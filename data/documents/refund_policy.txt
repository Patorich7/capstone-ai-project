TORILO ACADEMY REFUND POLICY

DOCUMENT STATUS
Authority: Synthetic project document
Status: Approved for capstone demonstration
Document Type: Student Policy

PURPOSE

This document provides project-level guidance concerning student refund requests.

REFUND REQUESTS

Students who wish to ask about a refund should contact the appropriate academy support or programme administration channel.

A student should provide sufficient information about the course and payment when requesting assistance with a refund.

REFUND ELIGIBILITY

Refund eligibility should be determined according to the applicable academy refund procedure.

The chatbot must not promise that a refund will be approved unless the applicable policy clearly supports that conclusion.

REFUND PROCESSING

Questions about the status of a refund request should be directed to the appropriate academy support or programme administration channel.

The chatbot should not invent a refund processing period that is not contained in the approved knowledge base.

REFUND ARRIVAL

Students should not be given a specific refund arrival date unless an approved academy document provides such information.

Students with questions about when a refund will arrive should contact the appropriate academy support channel.

REFUND QUESTIONS

Students who are uncertain about refund eligibility, processing or status should contact the appropriate academy support or programme administration channel.

POLICY LIMITATION

This is a synthetic project document created for the Torilo Academy AI Engineering Student Assistant Chatbot capstone demonstration.

It should not be presented as an official published Torilo Academy refund policy unless official documentation is subsequently provided.
